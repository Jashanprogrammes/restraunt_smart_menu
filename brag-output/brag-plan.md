# Brag Plan: SAVOR — Artisanal Dining & Table Arcade

## What is this app?
SAVOR is a dark-luxury digital restaurant menu and table arcade designed for physical 8 × 13 cm matte black NFC + QR plaques, featuring 4 playable canvas physics mini-games, backend-less Telegram floor staff alerts, and zero-commission instant UPI table payments.

## The angle
"Ditch ₹50,000/month tablet POS hardware and clunky PDF QR menus."
A high-craft physical plaque experience that turns waiting for food into table arcade duels, dispatches waiter calls directly to staff Telegram in real-time, and settles bills instantly over UPI — running on 100% free serverless infrastructure (₹0.00 forever).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Extreme close-up on an 8 × 13 cm physical matte black & gold plaque resting on dark smoked wood.
A smartphone taps the gold NFC coil: a tactile ripple pulses outward as the screen awakens directly to **TABLE 04 // FIRE, COAL & SALT**.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Culinary Canvas (Scene 2)**: 48-hour cold-fermented sourdough, Old Delhi coal butter chicken simmered on live embers. Sharp brutalist 0px luxury aesthetics with zero marketing fluff.
- **Table Arcade // Time Pass (Scene 3)**: Food takes 14 minutes. Instead of doom-scrolling, guests play canvas-physics Burger Stack, Memory Match, and Pizza vs Burger table duels right at Table 04.
- **Zero-Fee Infrastructure (Scene 4)**: 
  - Floor waiter call dispatches instant Telegram HTML box alerts to staff phones.
  - Settle bill instantly via dynamic client-side UPI QR code + 1-tap GPay / PhonePe deep link.

## Outro / punchline
"Total hardware cost: ₹200 for the plaque. Total monthly server fees: ₹0.00."
Logo lockup: SAVOR — Artisanal Dining & Table Arcade.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Physical NFC plaque tap verifies Table 04 cryptographically (`?t=sv-i9752`).
2. **Key Action**: Guest browses woodfired signatures, builds a physics burger in the arcade while waiting, and taps "Call Waiter".
3. **Result**: Telegram bot alerts staff floor captain in <200ms; guest scans dynamic UPI QR or taps 1-click UPI to settle the check.
